# AIMS Starter Kit for ISO/IEC 42001:2023

Free, editable templates for an AI management system (AIMS) under **ISO/IEC 42001:2023**, from Brivellis. No sign-up.

**Version 1.0 · November 2026 · 16 files (Word and Excel) · Free for use within one organisation**

Kit page: https://brivellis.com/resources/aims-starter-kit-iso-42001/

## What it is

A complete, connected starting set for an AIMS: context, roles and scope, an AI system inventory, an AI policy with objectives, one method for AI risk and impact assessment, a risk register, an impact assessment, a Statement of Applicability for all 38 Annex A controls by number, a life-cycle record, an evidence register, an internal audit and management review pack, and a crosswalk to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, the NIST AI RMF 1.0 and ISO/IEC 27090.

The files work as one chain, so an auditor can follow any thread from start to finish: AI system inventory → risk and impact assessment → Statement of Applicability → evidence → internal audit → management review. The workbooks flag broken links, such as a High-tier AI system with no impact assessment, an excluded control that a risk still needs, or an applicable control with no evidence.

Every file carries a worked example for a fictional company, Falcon Ridge IT Services, and its six AI systems. In the documents the example sits in blue boxes; in the workbooks it sits in grey cells. Replace or delete it before you issue anything.

## How to use it

1. **Find your AI systems.** Name an AIMS owner and get top management to sponsor the work. Use the Where to Look tab of the inventory (02) and list every system, including AI features built into software you already use.
2. **Context, roles and scope (01).** Get it approved.
3. **AI policy, objectives and roles (03).** Get the policy approved and communicated.
4. **Risk and impact method (04).** Agree tiers, scales and who accepts what, before any scoring.
5. **Risk register and impact assessments (05, 06).** Tier every system, run impact assessments where the method requires them, then score, treat and accept risks.
6. **Controls in the Statement of Applicability (07).** Paste the risk links, decide every control, justify it, and record status and owner.
7. **Life-cycle records and evidence (08, 09).** Record how each system is run and where the evidence is.
8. **Internal audit and management review (10).** Run the AIMS for a few months, then audit and review.

Plan on about 60 to 90 days of part-time work for one AIMS owner to reach a first complete set of documents and records. A certification audit then expects to see the system operating for a period.

**Already run ISO/IEC 27001?** The two standards share the same management system structure, so document control, internal audit, management review and corrective action can serve both. Part (a) of the crosswalk (11) shows, clause by clause, what to reuse and what to add. The risk scales match the Brivellis ISMS kit, so one risk can be scored the same way in both registers.

**Using AI to tailor the kit?** Read 00a first and fill in the master context in 12 once. AI drafts; a named person reviews and approves, and each session goes in the AI-Use Log (13). Never use AI to set risk tiers or scores, impact conclusions, control applicability or status, or to create evidence, audit results or sign-offs.

## Before you use it

- You need a licensed copy of ISO/IEC 42001:2023. The kit refers to clause and control numbers with short descriptions in our own words; it does not reproduce the standard, the Annex A control titles or text, or the Annex B guidance. Official copies are sold by the ISO Store, the IEC Webstore and national standards bodies.
- Verify against the current edition. References were checked against ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (first edition, no amendment) in October 2026.
- The crosswalk is a Brivellis mapping, not an official ISO, IEC or NIST mapping. For each Annex A control it shows the NIST AI RMF 1.0 categories given in the NIST AI RMF to ISO/IEC 42001 crosswalk listed on the NIST AI Resource Center (written against the final draft of the standard), plus one Brivellis primary category. NIST AI RMF category text is quoted from NIST AI 100-1 (https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1); NIST does not endorse this kit.
- These are templates, not legal, certification or audit advice. Using them does not by itself make an organisation conform to ISO/IEC 42001:2023. Laws on AI, such as the EU AI Act, may add duties that an AIMS does not cover by itself; check those that apply to you.
